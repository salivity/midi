General MIDI 1 System Enable Command Sequence

This article provides the exact hexadecimal byte sequence for the classical General MIDI 1 (GM1) System Enable command. It details the precise Universal Non-Real Time System Exclusive message required to reset a synthesizer or sound module into GM1 compliance, explains the purpose of each individual byte in the payload, and outlines the standard operational state the command establishes on receiving hardware.

The Exact Hexadecimal Sequence

The definitive sequence of MIDI bytes constituting the classical General MIDI 1 System On (Enable) message is:

F0 7E 7F 09 01 F7

In decimal format, this translates to:

240 126 127 09 01 247

Byte-by-Byte Breakdown

The command is defined within the Universal System Exclusive (SysEx) specification established by the MIDI Manufacturers Association (MMA):

  • F0 (Exclusive Status): The standard MIDI status byte designating the start of a System Exclusive message.
  • 7E (Non-Real Time Header): Identifies the transmission as a Universal Non-Real Time System Exclusive message.
  • 7F (Target Device ID): The broadcast or "All Call" address. Setting this byte to 7F instructs every receiving device on the MIDI line to process the message regardless of their assigned individual device ID (values from 00 to 7E target a specific unit).
  • 09 (Sub-ID #1): Specifies the message category as General MIDI.
  • 01 (Sub-ID #2): The specific GM command function for "General MIDI 1 On" (Enable). For context, 02 corresponds to General MIDI Off, and 03 is General MIDI 2 On.
  • F7 (End of Exclusive - EOX): The terminating status byte that signals the end of the System Exclusive transmission.

System Reset Behavior and Operational Impact

When a GM-compatible device receives this sequence, it resets all internal sound generation parameters to baseline General MIDI 1 requirements:

  • Channel Allocation: Channels 1 through 9 and 11 through 16 are configured as standard melodic instrument tracks. Channel 10 is reserved exclusively for the GM percussion kit.
  • Program Changes: Melodic channels are typically reset to Program 1 (Acoustic Grand Piano), and Channel 10 defaults to the standard Drum Kit.
  • Controller States: Standard Control Change (CC) parameters are returned to their default states:
    • Volume (CC 7) resets to 100 (or 90 in older hardware implementations).
    • Expression (CC 11) resets to 127.
    • Pan (CC 10) resets to 64 (center).
    • Modulation (CC 1), Hold Pedal (CC 64), and effect sends reset to 0.
    • Pitch Bend resets to center (0 semi-tones), with the pitch bend range reset to ±2 semitones.
  • Polyphony and Modes: Receiver defaults to Omni Off / Poly mode (MIDI Mode 3) with a minimum capability of 24 dynamically allocated voices.

Practical Implementation Note

Sound modules require processing time to clear memory and reinitialize sound banks upon receiving this command. Sequencers and playback engines should provide a pause of at least 50 to 100 milliseconds immediately following the transmission of F0 7E 7F 09 01 F7 before sending additional Channel Voice or Control Change messages.
