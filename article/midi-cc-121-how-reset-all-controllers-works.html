MIDI CC 121: How Reset All Controllers Works

MIDI Continuous Controller 121 (CC 121), formally known as "Reset All Controllers," is a standard MIDI Channel Mode message designed to return a device's performance controls to their default states. When a synthesizer, virtual instrument, or hardware module receives CC 121, it neutralizes expressive alterations made during playback or performance without cutting off sounding notes or resetting patch selections. This article explains precisely how receiving a CC 121 command modifies expression, modulation, and pitch bend parameters according to the official MIDI specification.

Modulation Wheel (MIDI CC 1)

Upon receiving a CC 121 message, the modulation wheel controller (CC 1) resets immediately to a value of 0. In standard MIDI implementation, modulation is an additive depth controller used to introduce vibrato, tremolo, or filter sweeps. By resetting this value to zero, CC 121 turns off any active low-frequency oscillation (LFO) or modulation effects, returning the sound to its natural, unmodulated timbre.

Expression Controller (MIDI CC 11)

Expression (CC 11) is reset to its maximum value of 127 when CC 121 is executed. While Master Volume (CC 7) sets the overall level of a MIDI channel and is intentionally left untouched by CC 121, Expression acts as a percentage-based secondary volume control for dynamic shaping. Resetting CC 11 to 127 ensures the instrument is fully audible and not suppressed by an earlier dynamic dip or fade-out.

Pitch Bend

Pitch Bend is reset to its center position (raw value 8192, or a data byte pair of 0x00, 0x40). Because pitch bend is a high-resolution, bipolar 14-bit message rather than a standard 7-bit CC, it does not share a standard CC number. However, the MIDI specification explicitly mandates that CC 121 center the pitch bend wheel. This immediately eliminates any active pitch offset, ensuring subsequent notes play accurately in tune.

Summary of Parameter Changes

  • Modulation (CC 1): Set to 0 (minimum/off).
  • Expression (CC 11): Set to 127 (maximum/open).
  • Pitch Bend: Set to 8192 (center/no detune).

Practical Application in Music Production

CC 121 is primarily used by Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs) when stopping playback or looping sections to prevent "stuck" expressive states. If a track stops while the pitch bend is pushed up or the expression is automated down, CC 121 instantly restores the channel to neutral. This prevents unexpected detuning, unwanted vibrato, or silent playback when auditioning instruments or restarting playback from a new position in the timeline.
