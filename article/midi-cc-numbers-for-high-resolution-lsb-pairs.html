MIDI CC Numbers for High-Resolution LSB Pairs

Standard MIDI Continuous Controller (CC) messages typically provide 7-bit resolution, yielding 128 discrete steps. To achieve smoother adjustments, the MIDI specification allows 14-bit high-resolution control by pairing two 7-bit controllers together as Most Significant Byte (MSB) and Least Significant Byte (LSB) values. This article identifies the exact MIDI CC numbers permanently designated to handle LSB data for these high-resolution pairs and explains how they function within the MIDI specification.

The Designated LSB Range: CC 32 to CC 63

In the MIDI 1.0 specification, MIDI CC 32 through CC 63 are permanently assigned as the LSB counterparts for primary controllers.

These numbers directly mirror the first 32 continuous controllers (CC 0 through CC 31), which serve as the MSB. To calculate the corresponding LSB CC number for any primary controller in the 0–31 range, you simply add 32:

\[\text{LSB CC Number} = \text{MSB CC Number} + 32\]

Common MSB and LSB Controller Pairs

When paired, the MSB and LSB combine to produce 16,384 possible values (\(2^{14}\)), eliminating audible stepping or "zipper noise" during parameter sweeps. Below are some of the most common standard pairings:

  • Bank Select: CC 0 (MSB) and CC 32 (LSB)
  • Modulation Wheel: CC 1 (MSB) and CC 33 (LSB)
  • Breath Controller: CC 2 (MSB) and CC 34 (LSB)
  • Foot Controller: CC 4 (MSB) and CC 36 (LSB)
  • Portamento Time: CC 5 (MSB) and CC 37 (LSB)
  • Channel Volume: CC 7 (MSB) and CC 39 (LSB)
  • Balance: CC 8 (MSB) and CC 40 (LSB)
  • Pan: CC 10 (MSB) and CC 42 (LSB)
  • Expression Controller: CC 11 (MSB) and CC 43 (LSB)

Transmission and Parsing Rules

When transmitting 14-bit dual-CC data, standard hardware and software implementations follow specific operational rules:

  1. Order of Transmission: The MSB is typically transmitted first to establish the coarse value, followed immediately by the LSB to deliver the fine adjustment.
  2. Coarse Adjustments Only: If a parameter only requires standard 7-bit precision, sending the MSB (CC 0–31) alone is permissible; the receiving device assumes an LSB value of 0.
  3. Fine Adjustments Only: If fine-tuning is required without changing the coarse position, the LSB (CC 32–63) can be transmitted independently after the initial MSB has been set.
