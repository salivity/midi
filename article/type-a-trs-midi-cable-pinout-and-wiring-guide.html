Type A TRS MIDI Cable Pinout and Wiring Guide

This guide details the specific wiring pinout used in a Type A TRS (Tip-Ring-Sleeve) MIDI cable. As the official standard adopted by the MIDI Manufacturers Association (MMA), Type A wiring interfaces 3.5mm (1/8") or 6.35mm (1/4") TRS jacks with traditional 5-pin DIN MIDI connectors. Below is the precise pin-to-connector mapping required for constructing or troubleshooting a Type A TRS MIDI connection.

Type A TRS to 5-Pin DIN Wiring Specification

In standard 5-pin DIN MIDI connections, only three pins are used: Pin 2 (Ground/Shield), Pin 4 (Source / + / VCC), and Pin 5 (Sink / - / Data). A Type A TRS MIDI cable connects these pins to the jack contacts as follows:

  • Tip: Connects to Pin 5 (MIDI Data / Current Sink)
  • Ring: Connects to Pin 4 (MIDI Power / Current Source, +5V or +3.3V through a resistor)
  • Sleeve: Connects to Pin 2 (Ground / Shield)

Identifying 5-Pin DIN Pin Numbers

When looking directly at the face of a standard female 5-pin DIN MIDI port (or the rear solder lugs of a male plug), the 180-degree pin layout is numbered non-sequentially from left to right:

  • Pin 1 (Unused)
  • Pin 4 (Source / Ring)
  • Pin 2 (Shield / Sleeve — Center Pin)
  • Pin 5 (Data / Tip)
  • Pin 3 (Unused)

Pins 1 and 3 are omitted from the TRS connection entirely, as they are not used in standard unidirectional MIDI communication.
